# Field Data Handling and Research Ethics

## Before fieldwork
- Confirm institutional approval, permits, and supervisor requirements.
- Explain the study and obtain informed consent where required.
- Collect only information needed for the research question.
- Use participant codes rather than names in working datasets where appropriate.

## During fieldwork
- Record observations accurately and distinguish direct observation from reported information.
- Do not pressure participants to answer.
- Avoid recording unnecessary personal identifiers.
- Follow safety rules and wildlife-distance guidance.

## Before public sharing
- Remove names, phone numbers, household identifiers, signatures, and exact sensitive coordinates.
- Aggregate or anonymize information only where the research approvals and consent allow it.
- Check that small groups cannot be re-identified from combined details.
- Never disclose locations that could put vulnerable wildlife at risk.
