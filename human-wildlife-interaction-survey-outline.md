# Human-Wildlife Interaction Survey Outline

> This is a planning outline, not an approved questionnaire. Obtain supervisor/ethics approval and adapt it to the study before collecting data.

## Introduction and consent
- Introduce the researcher and institution.
- Explain the purpose, voluntary nature, expected duration, and confidentiality limits.
- Ask for informed consent using the approved procedure.

## Possible topic areas
1. General experience of human-wildlife interactions (without collecting unnecessary identifiers).
2. Types of interaction reported and approximate frequency/time period.
3. Perceived causes and impacts on livelihoods or safety.
4. Existing prevention, response, or reporting measures.
5. Community views on possible mitigation approaches.

## Closing
- Ask whether the participant has anything else they wish to share.
- Thank them and provide approved contact or referral details if applicable.

## Data minimization
Do not collect names, phone numbers, identity numbers, exact home coordinates, or other identifiers unless the approved protocol requires them and safeguards are in place.
