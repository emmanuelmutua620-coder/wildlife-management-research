- Community-based conservation
- Wildlife monitoring and field research
- Habitat and wildlife corridor management
- Conservation research and data collection

## Research Background

I hold a Diploma in Wildlife Management from the Wildlife Research and Training Institute (WRTI).

My interests include wildlife conservation, human-wildlife interactions, community engagement, and responsible field research.

## Responsible Research

All shared materials must respect research permissions, participant privacy, wildlife protection protocols, and institutional requirements.

Sensitive wildlife locations and identifiable participant information are not published.
