# Wildlife Management Research

This repository holds learning notes, blank fieldwork templates, and responsibly shareable research materials related to wildlife management, conservation, and human-wildlife interactions.

## Contents
- `templates/field-observation-template.csv` — blank field observation structure.
- `templates/human-wildlife-interaction-survey-outline.md` — draft question areas for a supervised study.
- `docs/field-data-handling-and-ethics.md` — data protection and responsible fieldwork notes.
- `case-study/CASE-STUDY.md` — document a completed, approved project.

## Focus areas
- Human-wildlife interactions
- Community-based conservation
- Habitat and corridor management
- Wildlife monitoring and field data
- Research ethics and responsible data handling

## Responsible sharing
Do not publish exact locations of vulnerable wildlife, unredacted participant information, identifiable household-level responses, or restricted field records. Follow institutional approval, consent, permit, and supervisor requirements for real research.
